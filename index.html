About Spinning Mule Records

Founded in 2018 by two music-obsessed Miami University alumni, Spinning Mule Records began as a passion project born out of late-night album listening sessions in the dorms. We wanted to create a cozy, nostalgic gathering space where students, faculty, and locals could step away from digital screens and reconnect with music in its most tangible form.

Why You’ll Love Digging Here:

  • Listening Stations: Try before you buy! Spin any used record at our vintage listening counter.
  • Student & Local Discounts: Show your Miami ID or local address for 10% off used vinyl every Tuesday.
  • Community Trade-Ins: Bring in your old collections for cash or generous store credit.
  • Curated Staff Picks: Handwritten reviews on album sleeves from our team of passionate audiophiles.
